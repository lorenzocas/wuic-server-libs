## 4. Toptal / Lemon.io application notes

- Apply to the **AI/ML track**, not the generic .NET track — the vetted rate is 2–3× higher and your RAG case study carries it.
- Lead with **outcomes and metrics** (hit@8 0.81, zero-downtime Angular 12→17), not a tech laundry list.
- Both case studies (RAG + Migration) go in the portfolio. The migration one is the door-opener; the RAG one is the premium differentiator.
- Expect screening: English/soft-skills → technical assessment → recorded interview. Be ready to talk through the side-by-side methodology and the LoRA reranker eval.
